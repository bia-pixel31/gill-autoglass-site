Accessibility

Last updated: July 2026

This statement reflects our ongoing commitment to accessibility. Saskatchewan does not currently mandate a specific accessibility standard for business websites, but we aim to meet WCAG 2.1 AA voluntarily.

Gill Auto Glass Ltd. is committed to making this website usable for everyone, including visitors using screen readers or other assistive technology. If something here gets in your way, we want to know so we can help and fix it.

What we aim for

We aim to follow the WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines where practical. In plain terms, that means:

  • Keyboard navigation: you can move through the menu, the quote form, the chat helper, and every button using only a keyboard, with a visible outline showing where you are.
  • Alt text on images: meaningful images carry text descriptions, and purely decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers so they don't add noise.
  • Sufficient colour contrast: text is set to meet AA contrast on our navy, green, and gold palette.
  • Respecting reduced-motion preferences: see below.

Motion and animation

The 3D glass animation in the header and the scroll effects automatically switch to a static version for visitors who have reduced motion enabled in their device settings. You do not need to do anything on our site; we read that preference from your device and adjust.

If something is hard to use

If any part of the site is difficult to use, contact us at gillautoglassyxe@gmail.com or 306-914-8760 (call or text) and we'll help directly or work to fix it. Tell us what you were trying to do and what got in the way, and we'll get back to you same-day or as soon as possible.
